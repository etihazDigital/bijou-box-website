Shipping Policy

Delivery Timelines

  • Standard Delivery: 3–5 working days across India
  • Express Delivery: 1–2 working days in select metro cities (additional charges apply)
  • Orders are processed Monday–Saturday, excluding public holidays

Shipping Charges

  • Free standard shipping on all prepaid orders above ₹1,999
  • A flat shipping fee applies below this threshold — shown at checkout
  • Cash on Delivery orders may carry a small COD handling fee

Order Tracking

You'll receive a tracking link by SMS/email once your order is dispatched. For any delay beyond the estimated window, message us on WhatsApp and we'll look into it right away.

Returns & Exchange Policy

We want you to love what you ordered. If something isn't right, here's how returns and exchanges work.

Exchange Window

  • Exchanges are accepted within 7 days of delivery
  • Items must be unused, unworn and returned in original packaging with all tags intact
  • Earrings and any pierced/hygiene-sensitive items cannot be exchanged, for hygiene reasons

How To Request An Exchange

  • Message us on WhatsApp or email hello@thebijoubox.in with your order number and reason
  • We'll arrange a reverse pickup where available, or share a return address
  • Once received and inspected, your exchange or store credit will be processed within 5–7 working days

Damaged Or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged or incorrect, let us know within 48 hours of delivery with photos — we'll prioritise a replacement or refund.
