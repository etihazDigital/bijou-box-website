Our Story

Jewellery for real, everyday moments.

How We Started

Beautiful jewellery shouldn't need a special occasion.

The Bijou Box began with a simple frustration: the jewellery that looked the most beautiful was almost never the jewellery you could actually live in. Fine pieces stayed in the box, saved for "someday," while everyday jewellery faded, tarnished or turned your skin green within a week.

So we set out to build something in between — demi-fine jewellery with the look of fine gold, the price of fast fashion, and the durability to survive an actual life. Every Bijou Box piece is 18K gold plated over a waterproof, anti-tarnish base, tested to handle showers, humidity and the Mumbai monsoon alike.

Today, thousands of customers wear their Bijou Box pieces every single day — in the shower, at the beach, on ordinary Tuesdays — and that's exactly the point.
